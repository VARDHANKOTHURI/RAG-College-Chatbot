# Section 2: Admissions & Eligibility Criteria

Admissions at Greenwood Institute of Technology are strictly merit-based through state/national entrance examinations and authorized management quotas.

### Undergraduate (B.Tech) Programs & Seats
1. Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) — Intake: 240 seats
2. CSE with Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (CSE-AIML) — Intake: 180 seats
3. CSE with Data Science (CSE-DS) — Intake: 120 seats
4. Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE) — Intake: 180 seats
5. Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE) — Intake: 120 seats
6. Mechanical Engineering (MECH) — Intake: 60 seats
7. Civil Engineering (CIVIL) — Intake: 60 seats

### Eligibility for B.Tech Admissions
- Candidates must have passed 10+2 / Intermediate Examination with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry (MPC) with a minimum aggregate score of 60% (50% for reserved categories).
- Valid rank in state entrance exam (EAMCET/CET) or JEE Main.
- Management quota admissions require registration through the college portal with a minimum 70% aggregate in Class 12 PCM.

### Documents Required at the Time of Admission
1. 10th / SSC Marks Memo (Original + 3 Copies)
2. 10+2 / Intermediate Marks Memo (Original + 3 Copies)
3. Transfer Certificate (TC) and Conduct Certificate from previous institution
4. Entrance Exam Rank Card & Hall Ticket
5. Caste/Category Certificate and Income Certificate (if applying for fee reimbursement)
6. Aadhaar Card copy and 6 Passport-size color photographs
7. Migration Certificate (for students from boards outside the state)

# Section 3: Fee Structure & Payment Policies

All academic fees must be remitted exclusively through the official college ERP portal (https://erp.greenwood.edu) via Net Banking, UPI, or Demand Draft drawn in favor of "Greenwood Institute of Technology" payable at Hyderabad.

### Annual Academic Tuition Fees (2026-2027)
- B.Tech (CSE, CSE-AIML, CSE-DS, ECE): INR 1,45,000 per academic year.
- B.Tech (EEE, MECH, CIVIL): INR 1,15,000 per academic year.
- M.Tech (All Specializations): INR 90,000 per academic year.
- MBA & MCA Programs: INR 85,000 per academic year.

### One-Time & Recurring Ancillary Fees
- One-Time Admission & Registration Fee: INR 10,000 (non-refundable)
- Refundable Laboratory & Library Caution Deposit: INR 10,000 (refundable upon graduation/clearing no-dues)
- Annual Student Activities, Sports & Cultural Fee: INR 5,000
- Examination Fee per Semester: INR 2,200 per regular semester; supplementary exam fee is INR 500 per paper.

### Payment Deadlines & Late Penalties
- Odd Semester Tuition Fee: Due by July 31st each year.
- Even Semester Tuition Fee: Due by December 31st each year.
- Grace Period: 10 days with a late fine of INR 500; payments delayed beyond 30 days attract an additional penalty of INR 50 per day of delay.

# Section 4: Scholarships & Financial Assistance

Greenwood Institute offers comprehensive merit and need-based financial aid to support meritorious students.

### Institutional Merit Scholarships
1. **Chairman's Platinum Excellence Scholarship**: 100% Tuition Fee Waiver for candidates with State Entrance Rank below 500 or JEE Main Percentile above 98.0.
2. **Dean's Gold Scholarship**: 50% Tuition Fee Waiver for candidates with State Entrance Rank between 501 and 1,500.
3. **Academic Consistency Award**: INR 25,000 cash grant for students securing Semester SGPA > 9.50 in any regular semester.

### Need-Based & Special Financial Aid
- **Merit-cum-Means Grant**: Up to 40% tuition assistance for students whose annual family income is below INR 2,50,000 and maintain minimum SGPA of 7.50.
- **Sports Quota Scholarship**: 50% fee concession for national-level sports medalists; 25% for state-level representatives.
- **State Government Fee Reimbursement**: Eligible SC/ST/BC/EBC/Minority students receive full or partial tuition reimbursement as per state government welfare guidelines.

# Section 5: Residential Hostel Facilities & Guidelines

The campus provides separate on-campus accommodation blocks for boys (Nilgiri & Aravalli Blocks) and girls (Sahyadri & Vindhya Blocks) accommodating over 2,200 students.

### Hostel Fee Structure (Annual 2026-2027)
- 4-Sharing Standard Room (Non-AC): INR 75,000 per year (includes 4 meals daily).
- 3-Sharing Deluxe Room (Non-AC): INR 88,000 per year.
- 2-Sharing Premium Room (AC): INR 1,20,000 per year (electricity charged as per sub-meter consumption).
- Refundable Hostel Caution Deposit: INR 8,000 (one-time).

### Hostel Rules & Discipline
1. Night Curfew: All hostel residents must enter their respective hostel blocks before 9:00 PM on weekdays and 9:30 PM on weekends.
2. Biometric Attendance: Recorded daily between 8:30 PM and 9:30 PM.
3. Outing & Leave Permission: Gate passes must be applied online via the College ERP at least 24 hours in advance and requires digital approval from the parent/guardian.
4. Prohibited Items: Electric heaters, immersion rods, induction stoves, intoxicating substances, alcohol, and tobacco are strictly prohibited. Possession will result in immediate expulsion from the hostel.
5. Mess Menu: South Indian, North Indian, and continental breakfast options available daily with hygienic vegetarian and non-vegetarian (3 days/week) spreads.

# Section 7: Student Clubs, Cultural Events & Technical Fests

Campus life at GIT offers a dynamic blend of technical innovation, creative expression, and sports leadership.

### Major Annual Festivals
- **TechSpectra**: Annual National Level Technical Symposium held in March. Features hackathons, robotics combat, paper presentations, and coding marathons with cash pools exceeding INR 5,00,000.
- **Rhythm & Resonance**: 3-day Annual Cultural Fest celebrated in October featuring music concerts, dance battles, drama, and celebrity performances.

### Active Student Clubs
1. **Google Developer Student Club (GDSC)**: Focuses on Android, Cloud, Web development, and AI workshops. (Coordinator: gdsc@greenwood.edu)
2. **ACM Student Chapter**: Competitive programming, algorithmic problem solving, and ACM-ICPC training.
3. **Robotics & IoT Club (RoboTech)**: Drones, embedded systems, IoT sensors, and autonomous rover projects.
4. **Literary & Debating Society (LitSoc)**: MUNs, parliamentary debates, creative writing, and public speaking.
5. **Aura Dance & Music Crew**: Classical and contemporary cultural performance troupe.
6. **National Service Scheme (NSS) & Green Club**: Community service, blood donation camps, and environmental plantation drives.

# Section 8: Central Library & Digital Resources

The Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Central Library is a fully automated knowledge resource center with RFID technology.

### Resources & Collections
- Physical Book Titles: Over 85,000 volumes covering Engineering, Management, Sciences, and Humanities.
- Digital Library: 60 high-speed terminals with access to IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, SpringerLink, ACM Digital Library, and Scopus indexed journals.
- NPTEL & SWAYAM Local Chapter: Access to video lectures, lecture notes, and certification course materials.

### Library Rules & Borrowing Privileges
- Undergraduate Students: 4 books for a duration of 14 days, renewable once if no reservation exists.
- Postgraduate & Research Scholars: 6 books for 30 days.
- Overdue Fine: INR 2 per book per day for the first week; INR 5 per day thereafter.
- Quiet Zones: Absolute silence must be maintained in Reading Halls 1 & 2. Mobile phones must remain on silent mode.
