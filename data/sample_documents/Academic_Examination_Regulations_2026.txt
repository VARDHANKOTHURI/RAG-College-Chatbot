# Section 1: Attendance Requirements & Condonation Rules

Attendance in all theory lectures, tutorial classes, and laboratory practical sessions is mandatory.

### Minimum Attendance Criteria
1. A student is eligible to appear for the End-Semester Examinations (ESE) only if they secure a minimum aggregate attendance of 75% in all registered subjects of the semester.
2. **Condonation of Attendance Shortage**:
   - The Principal/Dean may condone a shortage of attendance between 65% and 74.9% on genuine medical grounds (supported by medical certificate from a registered physician within 3 working days of absence) or representing the college in state/national academic or sports competitions.
   - Condonation Fee: INR 1,500 per semester.
   - Attendance below 65% CANNOT be condoned under any circumstances. Students with < 65% attendance will be detained for the semester and must repeat the semester in the subsequent academic year.

# Section 2: Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) & Semester End Exams (SEE)

Evaluation in each course is divided into Continuous Internal Evaluation (40 Marks) and Semester End Examination (60 Marks), totaling 100 Marks per course.

### Internal Assessment Breakdown (40 Marks)
- Mid-Term Examination 1 (Descriptive + Objective): 15 Marks (Conducted in 8th week)
- Mid-Term Examination 2 (Descriptive + Objective): 15 Marks (Conducted in 16th week)
- Continuous Assignments / Quizzes: 5 Marks
- Seminar / Class Participation: 5 Marks
- Average or best-of policy: Weightage of 80% for the best mid-term score + 20% for the second mid-term score.

### Passing Minimum Standards
- A student must secure at least 35% (21 marks out of 60) in the Semester End Examination.
- A student must secure an overall minimum of 40% (40 marks out of 100 combined CIE + SEE) to earn credits in a theory course.
- For Practical / Lab courses: Minimum 50% in CIE and 50% in External Lab Viva/Exam.

# Section 3: Grading System & GPA Calculation

The institution adopts a 10-point absolute letter grading system in accordance with UGC Choice Based Credit System (CBCS).

### Letter Grades & Grade Points
- **O (Outstanding)**: 90% to 100% | Grade Point: 10.0
- **A+ (Excellent)**: 80% to 89% | Grade Point: 9.0
- **A (Very Good)**: 70% to 79% | Grade Point: 8.0
- **B+ (Good)**: 60% to 69% | Grade Point: 7.0
- **B (Above Average)**: 50% to 59% | Grade Point: 6.0
- **C (Pass)**: 40% to 49% | Grade Point: 5.0
- **F (Fail)**: Less than 40% | Grade Point: 0.0 (Backlog)
- **Ab (Absent)**: Grade Point: 0.0

### Award of Degree Classifications
- **First Class with Distinction**: CGPA >= 8.00 with all courses cleared in the first attempt without any history of backlogs.
- **First Class**: CGPA >= 6.75
- **Second Class**: 5.75 <= CGPA < 6.75
- **Pass Class**: 5.00 <= CGPA < 5.75

# Section 4: Examination Hall Tickets, Revaluation & Challenge Valuation

### Hall Ticket Issuance
- Hall tickets are issued digitally 7 days prior to examination commencement on the ERP portal.
- Students must have cleared all tuition fees, hostel dues, and library book loans to download their hall ticket.
- Carrying a physical printed hall ticket and college student ID card inside the examination hall is mandatory.

### Revaluation & Photocopy of Answer Scripts
- Students dissatisfied with their results may apply for **Recounting** or **Revaluation** within 10 days of result declaration.
- Recounting Fee: INR 300 per paper.
- Revaluation Fee: INR 1,200 per paper.
- Photocopy of Evaluated Answer Script: INR 800 per paper.
- If revaluation results in a mark change greater than 15% of maximum marks, the paper is referred to a third evaluator and the new average is awarded.
- **Challenge Valuation**: INR 10,000 per paper. If the student gets an increase of 20% or more, 80% of the challenge fee (INR 8,000) is refunded to the student.
